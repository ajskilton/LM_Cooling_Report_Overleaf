\subsection{Experiment 1: Thermal Comparison}

At the flow rates achieved, Galinstan cooled the resistors less effectively than
water. \Cref{tab:tn_results} compares the tests to the predicted temperatures from the thermal network models created in \cref{sec:thermal-model}.

             & \multicolumn{2}{c}{$T_\mathrm{out}$ (\si{\celsius})} \\

The model predicts $T_\mathrm{M}$ within $\pm\SI{2.2}{\celsius}$ measurement error~\cite{loh_project_2025} in three of the six tests (\cref{tab:tn_results}). \SI{1.4}{mL/s} Galinstan sits just outside the $\pm\SI{2.2}{\celsius}$ at \SI{2.6}{\celsius}. The two water tests below \SI{1}{\litre\per\minute} are over-predicted by 3.0 and \SI{3.6}{\celsius}.

Looking at the resistance models(\cref{fig:galinstan_rcp}--\ref{fig:water_rcp}, the total resistance with Galinstan was 0.49--\SI{0.80}{\celsius\per\watt}, against
0.26--\SI{0.27}{\celsius\per\watt} for water. Since the package resistance
$\Rpack \approx \SI{0.24}{\celsius\per\watt}$ is common to every test, the
difference lies in Galinstan's measured $R_{cp}$ being 10 to 35 times larger than water. 
Aside from water tests within the laminar region,  the models here also seem to show good correlation, 
though the limited data points restrict the certainty of the model's accuracy. 

There are several reasons the two laminar water tests may be inaccurate. First, the flow is not fully developed before entering the plate, so the turbulence caused by the pump or flow resistor required to achieve reduced flow rates improves heat transfer. Second, the flow meter measurement is outside its measurement range; hence, the flow measurements themselves could be off considerably.

The higher-flow laminar test has higher power; hence, any errors in the thermal resistance could cause the temperature to be overstated. A possible error here is that the isothermal plate assumption may not be accurate. As a result, the fictitious cold plate temperature is not a good assumption, as heat can flow more quickly to the much cooler areas around the inlet, whereas the model assumes flow to the outlet temperature.
