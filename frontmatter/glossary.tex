\chapter*{Glossary of Terms}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Glossary of Terms}

\begin{longtable}[l]{>{\raggedright}p{0.25\textwidth}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{0.68\textwidth}}
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Definition} \\
\addlinespace
Caloric-limited & Cold-plate regime ($\NTU \gtrsim 3$) in which the coolant leaves at almost the plate temperature, so heat removal is limited by the heat-capacity rate of the flow, $\mdot\,\cp$. \\
\addlinespace
Caloric resistance & Coolant temperature rise per unit heat load, $1/(\rho\,\cp\,\Vdot)$; the part of the thermal resistance set by the coolant's heat-capacity rate. \\
\addlinespace
Coefficient of performance & Ratio of the heat removed by a cooling loop to the power drawn by its pump. \\
\addlinespace
Cold plate & Metal plate with internal coolant passages onto which heat-dissipating components are mounted. \\
\addlinespace
Conduction pump & Electromagnetic pump that drives a conductive liquid by passing a DC current through it across a magnetic field, producing a Lorentz force. \\
\addlinespace
Conjugate heat transfer & Simulation that solves heat conduction in the solid and convection in the fluid together, with the heat flux and temperature matched at their interface. \\
\addlinespace
Convection-limited & Cold-plate regime ($\NTU \lesssim 1$) in which the coolant barely warms, so heat removal is limited by convection at the channel wall, $hA$. \\
\addlinespace
Eddy current & Current induced in a conductor by a time-varying magnetic field, dissipating power as heat. \\
\addlinespace
Effectiveness & Ratio of the heat actually absorbed by the coolant to the maximum possible for the given inlet and wall temperatures. \\
\addlinespace
Galinstan & Eutectic gallium--indium--tin alloy that is liquid at room temperature. \\
\addlinespace
Leakage field & Magnetic field of a magnetic component or IPT pad that extends outside its core into the surrounding space. \\
\addlinespace
Litz wire & Conductor made of many individually insulated strands, used to reduce AC resistance at high frequency. \\
\addlinespace
Package resistance & Thermal resistance from the resistor through its package and the thermal interface material to the cold plate, $\Rpack$. \\
\addlinespace
Power density & Power processed by a converter per unit volume. \\
\addlinespace
Skin depth & Depth at which an alternating current or field in a conductor has decayed to $1/e$ of its surface value. \\
\addlinespace
Thermal resistance & Temperature difference per unit heat flow between two points, in \si{\celsius\per\watt}. \\
\end{longtable}
